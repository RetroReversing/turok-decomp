# Provenance and licensing

This repository contains recovered and source-assisted C, address/type metadata, and build tooling for an unfinished matching decompilation. The initial checkpoint was recovered from a user-supplied ROM. Later checkpoints reuse and adapt existing original Turok source and headers; they are not independent ROM-only recovery. ROMs, extracted binary assets, and compiler binaries are not included. See docs/SOURCE_ASSISTED_CHECKPOINT.md for provenance.

Turok and the original game code/data belong to their respective rights holders. This repository does not grant rights to the original game or claim a blanket license over recovered game code. Supply your own supported ROM to generate local inputs and output.

`build.py` is generated from RetroReversing/decompdecomp, a continuation of NDelic's decompdecomp and Sam Wolfs' decomp.wtf. Its source is available at the revision recorded in README.md and is covered by AGPL-3.0; the license text is included at `tools/LICENSE-AGPL-3.0.txt`. The generated build tooling retains that license. `tools/prepare.py` is also provided under AGPL-3.0-only. The original project credits and licenses remain in the tool repository.

Machine drafts were produced using m2c and refined with compiler comparisons and decomp-permuter through decompdecomp. This provenance is preserved in the recovered source comments where applicable. These tools' licenses do not establish ownership or licensing of the original game.

The common assembly support headers were generated by splat/spimdisasm. Period compiler tools must be obtained separately through the toolchain registry and accepted under their applicable licenses; none are redistributed here.
